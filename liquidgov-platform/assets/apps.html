Get the app

One LiquidGov icon on your home screen. Not a store download: your phone turns this site into its own app.

LiquidGov

Your feed, messages, groups, the map and proposals, all in one place.

How to add it

iPhone: in Safari or Brave (not Chrome), tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open the browser's ⋮ menu, then Install app (or Add to Home screen).

If you added the old separate Messages, Social or Civic icons, they still open the right page. You can remove them and keep just this one.
