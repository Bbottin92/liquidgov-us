LiquidGov.US

This is genesis, not a government.

LiquidGov.US is a voluntary association and working software for liquid, natural-law democracy. Anyone here is a guest or a member of that association. Nobody on this site is your city, state, or federal government. No office seated from these pages has legal authority over anyone who did not choose to associate.

Website control is temporary. When a working election exists among enrolled citizens, that control is meant to end. The internal shape of that election is itself a citizen vote. Real governmental authority is a later, explicit landslide — Phase 2 — not this page and not the first people who show up.

Until then, genesis operators run the machines and say so in public. That is a disclosure, not a claim of sovereignty.

Three laws the project is built around: do not steal, do not damage, do not deprive of liberty. Voting weight, offices, land, and judgments live in a separate governance kernel. This website is the door, not the ledger.

Unverified visitors can look around. One combined verification is meant to unlock civic acts when that path exists. Minors are friends-and-family only. We will not treat a checkbox as age.
